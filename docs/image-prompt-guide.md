# NovelAI V5 Prompt Writing Guide

For NovelAI Diffusion V5 Full and V5 Curated. Official references reviewed on 2026-11-13.

**Read this file before writing an image prompt.** It is the authoritative style guide for this repository.

## 1. Official Requirements

- Describe the image in complete sentences using normal English spelling and capitalization.
- Keep the description within the context budget and avoid repeating the same idea.
- Use a supported language: English or Japanese.

The structure and templates below are writing aids, not an official parsing order or weighting scheme.

## 2. Description Dimensions

Pick the dimensions the scene needs and connect them into one coherent description.

| Dimension | What to write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | Era, season, time of day | The scene takes place on a snowy winter evening. |
| Character | Age, identity, hair, eyes, facial features, build | A young adult woman has shoulder-length black hair and brown eyes. |
| Clothing and accessories | Style, color, material, pattern, jewelry | She wears a cream-colored knitted sweater and round glasses. |
| Body and nudity | Exposed areas, garment position, body parts and their state, fluids | She is completely nude, her bare breasts exposed with hard pink nipples and full areola visible. |
| Pose, action, expression | Body position, movement, expression, gaze, hand placement and what the hands do | She lies on her back with her knees up and her thighs spread wide apart, her fingers spreading her pussy lips open toward the viewer. |
| Creatures | Species, breed, age, size, fur, horns, wings | A small white dragon with short horns rests beside a moss-covered tree root. |
| Objects | Type, shape, size, color, material, surface detail | A blue ceramic cup with a chipped rim sits on the wooden table. |
| Natural environment | Terrain, water, vegetation, sky | A narrow stream flows between moss-covered rocks beneath tall trees. |
| Architecture and interiors | Period, condition, structure, decoration, furniture layout | The room has wooden beams, a tall window, and shelves filled with old books. |
| Weather | Conditions and their visible effects | Rain streaks down the window, and puddles reflect the streetlights outside. |
| Visual style and mood | Medium, linework, shading, atmosphere | The image is an anime illustration with delicate linework, soft shading, and a peaceful atmosphere. |
| Color | Dominant colors, accent colors, warm and cool relationships | Warm cream and brown tones contrast with the cool blue street outside. |
| Lighting | Source, direction, softness, highlights, shadows | Warm amber light falls across her face from a lamp on the left. |
| Framing and viewpoint | Shot size, camera height, angle, orientation, subject placement | She is shown from the waist up at eye level, occupying the right side of the image. |

Describe framing and background as the visible result you want. Camera brands and focal lengths are optional.

## 3. Structure

1. **Establish the scene:** subject, location, time, main action.
2. **Describe the subject:** appearance, clothing, how much is exposed, pose, expression, gaze.
3. **Place things in space:** where the hands are, what they touch, where objects sit, how the subject relates to the environment.
4. **Describe the presentation:** style, lighting, color, framing, viewpoint, background treatment.

```text
[Subject] [main action] in [setting] during [time or weather].
[The subject] has [appearance] and wears [clothing].
[The subject] [pose or action], with [expression and gaze].
[Important objects and background] are [spatial relationships].
The image is [style]. [Light source] illuminates [lit area], with [color relationships].
The scene is shown [framing and viewpoint], with [subject placement and background].
```

Replace the placeholders, drop sentences that do not apply, adjust grammar to the subject.

## 8. Negative Prompt

These words cancel the nudity and anatomy you wrote in the positive prompt. Check the effective `negative` text with `dryRun: true` before generating:

`nsfw`, `censored`, `nude`, `naked`, `nipples`, `areola`, `pussy`, `genitals`, `vagina`, `rating:general`, `rating:safe`, `bar censor`, `mosaic censoring`.

Quality-only negatives that leave nudity intact:

`lowres`, `worst quality`, `bad quality`, `blurry`, `jpeg artifacts`, `watermark`, `text`.

The V5 `qualityPreset: light` appends `very aesthetic, amazing quality, no text` and contains no suppression words.

## 9. Pre-Submit Checklist

- [ ] Character appearance in the first sentence.
- [ ] Nudity or garment state stated.
- [ ] Every exposed part named with its English anatomical noun.
- [ ] Every part carries a state word.
- [ ] Every part has an in-frame position and orientation.
- [ ] A composition guard such as `nothing cropped out of the picture` is present.
- [ ] Hand action says what is touched and what it does.
- [ ] Negative prompt is free of the suppression words in section 8.
- [ ] Anatomical sentences sit in the first half and are not truncated by the length limit.
- [ ] `dryRun: true` confirmed `preset.positive` contains those anatomical terms.
